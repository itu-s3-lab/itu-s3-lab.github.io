Welcome to the new website of the **Smart Sensing and Systems Lab (S³ Lab)**!

We rebuilt the site from scratch to better tell what we do and to keep you up to date with life in the lab. Here is what's new:

- **A new look and our new logo**, designed to be clean, fast and readable on any device.
- **Research**, **Publications** and **Team** pages, so you can quickly see what we work on, what we publish and who we are.
- **Monthly recaps**: every month we will publish a short summary of papers, talks, new people and projects.
- **An RSS feed**, if you want our news delivered straight to your reader.

The previous version of the website is archived as release **v0.5** in our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/itu-s3-lab).

Stay tuned!
